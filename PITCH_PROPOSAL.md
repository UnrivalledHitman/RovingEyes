# Roving Eyes — Website Redesign Audit, Pricing Tiers & Pitch Playbook

Prepared for pitching **Roving Eyes** ([rovingeyes.com](https://www.rovingeyes.com/)) with a live working demo instead of a cold slide deck.

## 1. Live Site Audit Findings (The 6 Revenue Leaks on `rovingeyes.com`)

When pitching Roving Eyes, **do not pitch "a prettier website."** Pitch **fixing the broken collector funnel** on a gallery whose prints sell for **₹15,000–₹20,000** (`$260–$300`) each:

1. **Zero Print-to-Checkout Funnel (`0` forms, no cart, no CTA inside lightboxes):**  
   Clicking any photo opens a bare lightbox with no title, no reference code, and no *"Acquire Print"* button. Buyers have to memorize a photo code (e.g., `A04`), exit to a lowercase `buy` link, and manually email or dial a number.
2. **Non-Clickable Contact Numbers:**  
   `+91 98303 67890` and `+91 95990 91712` are plain text instead of instant `tel:` or pre-filled `https://wa.me/` WhatsApp order links.
3. **Heavy Center Watermarks Hurting Conversion:**  
   Every image carries a thick gray horizontal `© ROVING EYES` band straight across horizons, faces, and even the sample painting—making it hard for interior buyers to visualize the art on a wall. *(Our demo includes an interactive toggle comparing `Clean View`, `Subtle Corner Signature`, and `Old Center Band` so they feel the difference immediately.)*
4. **SmugMug Template & Footer Ownership Leak:**  
   The footer displays `© 2026 SmugMug, Inc.`, `Owner Log In`, and a `Contact` link that routes collectors to **SmugMug Help** instead of Roving Eyes.
5. **Mobile Above-the-Fold Collapse:**  
   On phones (`390×844`), excessive padding below the logo pushes the homepage slideshow nearly off-screen (`y ≈ 710px`), and oversized arrows overlap the small image.
6. **Hidden Hospitality Credibility & Incomplete Pricing Copy:**  
   High-trust credentials (placements at **ITC Ltd. – Hotels Division**, a **London restaurant chain**, and collectors in the US, UK, Dubai & New Zealand) are buried inside bio text, while paper print pricing, painting commission starting guides, and grammar/typos ("are on for sale", "Delivery wil be Free", "Odissa", "Budhist") erode luxury positioning.

## 3. Optional Care & Growth Retainer (`₹2,500/month` or `₹6,000/quarter`)

Small Indian businesses often resist open-ended monthly fees unless they see what they stop paying for elsewhere or what ongoing value they receive. Position it as **Optional** (never mandatory to sign the build):

- **What `₹2,500/month` covers:**
  - Uploading new expedition photos & applying subtle corner watermarks (`up to 25 new plates/month`)
  - Seasonal featured collections, price/INR-USD adjustments & exhibition banners
  - Form/WhatsApp routing uptime monitoring, SSL, analytics summary & priority same-day support
- **Quarterly Alternative (`₹6,000 / quarter`, saves ₹1,500):** Easier psychologically for boutique businesses than 12 monthly invoices.
- **SmugMug Cost Offset:** SmugMug paid plans typically cost ~\$15–\$38/month (`~₹15,000–₹38,000/year`) in recurring USD billing—without custom collector UX. Moving to static hosting on Vercel/Netlify (₹0 base tier) offsets most or all of a quarterly care plan.

## 4. ROI Justification (Why ₹30,000 Is Low Risk for Roving Eyes)

- Their standard **36"×24" stretched canvas sells for ₹20,000** (`$300` abroad), **24"×16" sells for ₹15,000** (`$260`), and **3×2 ft bespoke acrylic paintings** are higher-ticket commissions.
- **Just 2 canvas sales (`₹30,000–₹40,000`) pay back 100% of the Tier 2 flagship rebuild** — and every sale after that is pure upside on a site they own outright.
